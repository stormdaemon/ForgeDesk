# Code of conduct

ForgeDesk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and focus on
what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks
are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/stormdaemon/ForgeDesk/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are handled confidentially.
